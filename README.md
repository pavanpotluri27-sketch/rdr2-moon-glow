# Moon Glow for Red Dead Redemption 2

A huge, sharp moon, a big round sun, dense stars and the Milky Way, golden moon glitter on water, orange sunsets with black rider silhouettes - and **nine moon colours on a key**. Story mode. No ReShade, no filters: game settings only.

[![Moon Glow showcase](https://img.youtube.com/vi/JMgZC-SoS4s/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JMgZC-SoS4s)

**Download:** [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/reddeadredemption2/mods/11046) · [Releases](../../releases) · **Watch:** [showcase on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JMgZC-SoS4s)

## What it does
- Huge moon and big round sun, the same size as each other
- Dense stars and Milky Way, golden moon glitter on water
- Orange sunsets, red sky after the sun is down, black silhouettes against sun and moon
- Nine moon colours: Halloween, Christmas, Blood, Blue, Pink, Strawberry, Harvest, Green, Diwali
- Stop the clock on a moon night / sunset / sunrise, hold the moon where you want it, pick its shape, run the day as a time-lapse

## Keys (changeable in `SkyLock.ini`)
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| F11 | Sky lock: off > moon night > sunset > sunrise > off |
| Page Up / Page Down | While locked: roll the time forward / back |
| End | Hold the moon: low > half way > overhead > let go (PgUp/PgDn then change only its shape) |
| F3 | Next moon colour (night, 20:00 - 06:00) |
| F10 | Time-lapse on / off |

## Requirements
1. [Script Hook RDR2](http://www.dev-c.com/rdr2/scripthookrdr2/) (Alexander Blade) with its ASI loader (`dinput8.dll`)
2. [Lenny's Mod Loader (LML)](https://www.nexusmods.com/reddeadredemption2/mods/1472)

Tested on game version 1.0.1491.50.

## Install
1. Copy `SkyLock.asi` and `SkyLock.ini` next to `RDR2.exe`.
2. Copy `lml\MoonGlow` into the game's `lml\downloader` folder, open the LML Mod Manager and enable **Moon Glow** (keep all four options ticked).
3. Start the game and press **F11** in free roam - "Sky lock: MOON NIGHT" appears.

## Good to know
- Use only one timecycle / visual mod at a time.
- Switch the sky lock off (F11 until "off") before story missions.
- A moon colour tints the whole night picture, not only the moon.
- With DLSS on, dark shapes in front of the big moon can leave trails - DLSS off (TAA) and Motion Blur off fixes it.
- The sun is still being refined; tell me what you would change.
